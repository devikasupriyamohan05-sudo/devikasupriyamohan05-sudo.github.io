About

Hi, I'm Devika.

I'm an extroverted introvert. Some nights I'd rather stay home, cook something new, and lose myself in a TV show. Other times I'm happiest somewhere I've never been, meeting people I've just met and ordering things I can't pronounce. The second side has taken me to 33 countries so far, and I'm not planning to stop there.

I'm a statistics and data science student at Carnegie Mellon, minoring in business. This past summer I worked as an analyst at Frame Data & AI, where I helped build production AI systems for energy clients. What draws me to data is how widely it applies. The same skills can go toward healthcare research, energy, or the environmental problems I've cared about for years. My favorite work starts with a messy, ambiguous problem and ends with something people actually use.

Outside of school and work, I bake, collect vintage cameras, and get very invested in soccer when a game is on. I'm also always looking for the next great restaurant or café.

If any of this sparks a conversation, whether it's about a role, a project, or a restaurant recommendation, I'd love to hear from you.
